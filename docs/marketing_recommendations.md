# Marketing Recommendations

## Executive Summary

The analysis segments customers by value, recency, campaign response, and purchase channel behaviour. The goal is to help marketing teams target the right customers with the right offer instead of relying on broad campaigns.

## Recommended Actions

- Prioritise high-value responders for premium loyalty and cross-sell campaigns.
- Use win-back offers for high-value customers with high recency.
- Promote digital offers to customers with high web visits but low web purchases.
- Use product-specific bundles based on dominant category spend.
- Reduce broad campaign spend on low-value, low-response customers.

## Measurement Plan

- Use test/control groups for campaign targeting.
- Track incremental response rate and revenue uplift.
- Monitor unsubscribe or complaint rates after targeting.
- Review segment performance monthly.
