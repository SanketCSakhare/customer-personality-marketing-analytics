# Dashboard Requirements

## Audience

Marketing managers, CRM analysts, campaign managers, and data analyst hiring reviewers.

## Dashboard Pages

1. Customer Overview
2. Segment Performance
3. Campaign Response
4. Product Category Spend
5. Targeting Recommendations

## KPIs

- Customers
- Campaign response rate
- Average customer value
- Total product spend
- Average recency
- Web purchase share
- Store purchase share
- High-value customer count

## Required Slicers

- Customer segment
- Education
- Marital status
- Income band
- Has children
- Campaign response

## Acceptance Criteria

- Segment totals reconcile to customer-level data.
- Campaign response rate uses customer count as denominator.
- Product category totals reconcile to total customer value.
- Dashboard includes recommended targeting actions.
